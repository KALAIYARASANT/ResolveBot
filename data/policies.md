# ShopSphere Returns Policy

**Document ID:** returns_policy · **Version:** 3.0 · **Effective date:** 2025-04-01 · **Status:** Current

This version replaces the 2024 Returns Policy (returns_policy_2024). The 2024 version no longer applies to any order.

## §1 Scope

### §1.1 What this policy covers

This policy covers items bought on shopsphere.example.com and delivered in India.

### §1.2 Related policies

When and how refunds are paid is explained in the Refunds Policy (refunds_policy). Faults that appear after the return window are handled under the Warranty Policy (warranty_policy).

## §2 Return window

### §2.1 Standard return window

You can return most items within 30 days of the delivery date for any reason, including a change of mind.

### §2.2 How we count days

Day 0 is the delivery date shown in your order tracking. Day 30 is the last day on which you can start a return. We count calendar days, including weekends and public holidays.

## §3 Opened items

### §3.1 Returning opened items

Opened items can be returned within the standard return window (§2.1), the same as unopened items. The only exception is in-ear earphones and earbuds (§4.2).

## §4 Items that cannot be returned

### §4.1 Never returnable

Gift cards and software licences (digital or boxed) cannot be returned once bought.

### §4.2 Returnable only while sealed

In-ear earphones and earbuds can be returned only if the hygiene seal is unbroken. Once opened, they cannot be returned for a change of mind.

## §5 Defective, damaged or wrong items

### §5.1 Within the return window

If an item is defective or we sent the wrong item, you can return it within the standard return window (§2.1). This applies even to opened earphones and earbuds.

### §5.2 Damaged on arrival

Items that arrive damaged follow the Damaged or Defective Items Policy (damaged_or_defective_items), which has its own reporting deadline.

## §6 After the return window

### §6.1 Faults after the return window

Once the return window has ended, you cannot return an item for a refund. If the item develops a fault, you can make a warranty claim instead (see warranty_policy).

## §7 How to start a return

### §7.1 Return requests

Ask our support assistant or a support agent to create a return request. You need the order ID, the items you are returning and the reason.

### §7.2 Pickup

We send you a pickup slot by SMS within one business day. Please pack the item with all its accessories and manuals.

# ShopSphere Warranty Policy

**Document ID:** warranty_policy · **Version:** 2.2 · **Effective date:** 2025-06-01 · **Status:** Current

## §1 Scope

### §1.1 What this policy covers

ShopSphere gives a warranty on the products it sells against faults that appear during normal use.

## §2 Warranty periods

### §2.1 Standard warranty

Most products have a standard warranty of 12 months from the delivery date.

### §2.2 Laptops

Laptops have an extended warranty of 24 months from the delivery date.

### §2.3 Products without a warranty

Gift cards and software licences have no warranty.

### §2.4 How the end date is worked out

The warranty ends on the same calendar day, the stated number of months after the delivery date. For example, an item delivered on 15 March with a 12-month warranty is covered until 15 March of the next year. If that day does not exist in the end month, the warranty ends on the last day of that month. A claim made on the end date is valid.

## §3 What is covered

### §3.1 Covered faults

Manufacturing faults and parts that stop working during normal use, such as a screen that will not turn on, a port that stops charging or a battery that no longer holds a charge.

### §3.2 Not covered

Accidental damage, liquid damage, misuse, unauthorised repairs and cosmetic wear.

## §4 Making a claim

### §4.1 Warranty tickets

To make a claim, ask the support assistant to open a warranty support ticket. Give the order ID and a short description of the fault.

### §4.2 What happens next

Our service team contacts you within 2 business days to arrange inspection. Products under warranty are repaired or replaced. Warranty claims are not refunded.

# ShopSphere Shipping Policy

**Document ID:** shipping_policy · **Version:** 4.0 · **Effective date:** 2025-09-01 · **Status:** Current

## §1 Delivery

### §1.1 Delivery times

We deliver across India through partner carriers. Standard delivery takes 3–6 business days after dispatch.

### §1.2 Estimated delivery date

Every order shows an estimated delivery date (ETA) once it has been dispatched.

## §2 Shipping fees

### §2.1 Standard shipping fee

Standard shipping costs ₹99 per order. Orders with a subtotal of ₹2,999 or more ship free.

### §2.2 ShopSphere Plus

ShopSphere Plus members get free shipping on every order.

## §3 Tracking your order

### §3.1 Shipment statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `processing` | We are preparing your order. It has not left our warehouse. |
| `shipped` | The carrier has collected your order. |
| `in_transit` | Your order is moving through the carrier's network. |
| `out_for_delivery` | Your order will be delivered today. |
| `delivered` | Your order has been delivered. |
| `delayed` | Your order is late. The carrier has set a revised ETA. |

## §4 Delays

### §4.1 Revised ETA

If a shipment is delayed, the carrier sets a revised ETA. You can see it in your order tracking and the support assistant can tell you.

### §4.2 Delivery issues

If a delayed shipment is more than 3 days past its original ETA, you can email our support team to raise a delivery issue. Our logistics team will contact the carrier and update you within one business day.

## §5 Lost parcels

### §5.1 Reporting a lost parcel

If you believe your parcel is lost, email our support team and we will investigate with the carrier.

# Damaged or Defective Items Policy

**Document ID:** damaged_or_defective_items · **Version:** 1.3 · **Effective date:** 2025-04-01 · **Status:** Current

## §1 Scope

### §1.1 What this policy covers

This policy covers items that arrive damaged, items that are faulty and orders where we sent the wrong item.

## §2 Damaged on arrival

### §2.1 Reporting deadline

You must report damage within 48 hours of delivery. We count from the delivery date: each full calendar day after the delivery date counts as 24 hours. For example, a report made the day after delivery is 24 hours after delivery.

### §2.2 Your options

If you report damage in time, you can choose a replacement (when in stock) or a refund.

### §2.3 Late reports

Damage reported after the deadline is handled as a defect (see §3).

## §3 Defective items

### §3.1 Within the return window

A defective item can be returned within the standard return window (returns_policy §5.1).

### §3.2 After the return window

After the return window, a defective item is handled as a warranty claim (warranty_policy).

## §4 Wrong items

### §4.1 If we sent the wrong item

Return it within the standard return window, or ask us to send the correct item.

# ShopSphere Exchanges Policy

**Document ID:** exchanges · **Version:** 1.1 · **Effective date:** 2025-04-01 · **Status:** Current

## §1 Scope

### §1.1 What an exchange is

An exchange swaps an item for a different colour, size or variant of the same product.

## §2 Exchange window

### §2.1 When you can exchange

You can ask for an exchange within 30 days of the delivery date. The item must be unopened.

## §3 How exchanges are handled

### §3.1 Who handles exchanges

Exchanges are handled by our support team by phone or email. The chat assistant cannot process an exchange, but it can explain this policy or help you return the item instead.

### §3.2 Price differences

If the new variant costs more, you pay the difference. If it costs less, we refund the difference to your original payment method.

# Order Cancellation Policy

**Document ID:** order_cancellation · **Version:** 1.2 · **Effective date:** 2025-04-01 · **Status:** Current

## §1 When you can cancel

### §1.1 Before shipment

You can cancel an order while its status is "processing", which means it has not yet been shipped.

### §1.2 After shipment

Once an order has been shipped, it cannot be cancelled. You can return it after delivery under the Returns Policy (returns_policy).

## §2 How to cancel

### §2.1 Cancellation tickets

Ask the support assistant to open a cancellation ticket with your order ID. Our team confirms the cancellation by email.

## §3 Refunds for cancelled orders

### §3.1 Amount

A cancelled order is refunded in full, including any shipping fee.

### §3.2 Timing

The refund is issued within 3–5 business days of the cancellation.

## §4 Partial cancellation

### §4.1 Cancelling some items

We cannot cancel part of an order. You can cancel the whole order and place a new one.
